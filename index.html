How it works

Staff do three things. Your roster does the rest.

  1. 1

    Choose your week

    Staff log in with their email, pick the week and their name, and their shifts appear straight from your roster.

  2. 2

    Check your shifts

    Days, sites, times, hours and pay rate are already filled in. Staff only correct what's wrong.

  3. 3

    Sign and send

    Sign on screen, then download the PDF or share it straight to payroll from a phone.

What payroll gets

Less chasing, fewer corrections, and every timesheet in the same shape.

One format every time
Every timesheet arrives typed and laid out the same way, so there's nothing to decipher or retype.
Pay rates applied for you
Rates are set per role, with separate weekday and weekend rates, and you can set exceptions for individual people.
Less chasing
Staff can finish theirs in a few clicks from a phone, so fewer arrive late or incomplete.
Duplicate names sorted once
If the roster spells a name two ways, you merge them once and the fix applies to every week.

Set up around your roster

We configure each portal for your roster layout, roles and pay rules, so nothing changes in how you work.

Where your data lives

Staff details are personal data, so each company's stays separate from everyone else's.

  • No IT projectThere's nothing to install, and we never need access to your systems or your domain.
  • Encrypted connectionsEverything travels over HTTPS between staff devices and the portal.
  • Deleted when you askAsk us to remove one person's data or all of it, and it's deleted within 30 days.

Priced per person, quoted for your team

A small monthly fee for each member of staff using the portal, with a lower rate for larger teams. Tell us your headcount and we'll send a fixed quote.
